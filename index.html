How it works

Fresh dough every morning. Meat carved to order all day.

No dining room, no wine list, no website until now. Just a spit turning all day, samoon out of the oven every morning, and a queue out the door at lunchtime. Cash only — always has been.

Zero advertising. 1,100+ Google reviews and a second counter in Edgeley to show for it.
